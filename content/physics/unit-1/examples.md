# Solved Examples

## Example 1: Thin film bright fringe

A thin film has thickness $t = 250\,\text{nm}$, refractive index $\mu = 1.50$, and is viewed in reflected light with $r=0$. Find the condition for a bright fringe if the wavelength is $\lambda$.

Using the reflected-system condition:

$$
2\mu t\cos r + \frac{\lambda}{2} = m\lambda
$$

Since $r=0$, $\cos r = 1$:

$$
2\mu t + \frac{\lambda}{2} = m\lambda
$$

Rearrange:

$$
2\mu t = \left(m - \frac{1}{2}\right)\lambda
$$

This confirms that bright fringes occur for odd half-wave conditions in the reflected system.

## Example 2: ARC thickness calculation

A lens is coated with an anti-reflection film of refractive index $\mu_f = 1.38$ for light of wavelength $\lambda = 550\,\text{nm}$. What is the minimum coating thickness?

For a quarter-wave coating:

$$
 t = \frac{\lambda}{4\mu_f}
$$

Substitute values:

$$
 t = \frac{550\times 10^{-9}}{4\times 1.38}
   \approx 9.96\times 10^{-8}\,\text{m}
$$

So,

$$
 t \approx 100\,\text{nm}
$$

## Example 3: Malus's law

A polarizer and analyzer are set with an angle $\theta = 60^\circ$ between their transmission axes. If the initial intensity is $I_0$, what is the transmitted intensity?

By Malus's law:

$$
I = I_0\cos^2\theta
$$

$$
I = I_0\cos^2 60^\circ = I_0\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{I_0}{4}
$$

So the transmitted intensity is $25\%$ of the incident intensity.

## Example 4: Wedge fringe width

An air wedge has small angle $\alpha = 0.5^\circ$. If the wavelength is $600\,\text{nm}$, estimate the fringe width.

Use:

$$
\beta = \frac{\lambda}{2\alpha}
$$

with $\alpha$ in radians.

$$
0.5^\circ = 0.5\times \frac{\pi}{180} \approx 8.73\times 10^{-3}\,\text{rad}
$$

So:

$$
\beta = \frac{600\times 10^{-9}}{2\times 8.73\times 10^{-3}} \approx 3.44\times 10^{-5}\,\text{m}
$$

$$
\beta \approx 34\,\mu\text{m}
$$

# Wave Optics Formula Sheet

## Interference

- Constructive interference: $\Delta = n\lambda$
- Destructive interference: $\Delta = (2m+1)\frac{\lambda}{2}$
- Phase difference for maxima: $\phi = 2n\pi$
- Phase difference for minima: $\phi = (2m+1)\pi$

## Thin film (reflected light)

- Optical path difference: $\Delta = 2\mu t\cos r + \frac{\lambda}{2}$
- Maxima condition: $2\mu t\cos r = (2n\pm 1)\frac{\lambda}{2}$
- Minima condition: $2\mu t\cos r = n\lambda$

## Wedge-shaped film

- Optical path difference: $\Delta = 2\mu t\cos(r+\alpha)$
- Fringe width: $\beta = \frac{\lambda}{2\mu\sin\alpha}$
- For air and small $\alpha$: $\beta \approx \frac{\lambda}{2\alpha}$

## Anti-reflection coating

- Condition: $2\mu_f t = (2m+1)\frac{\lambda}{2}$
- Minimum practical thickness: $t = \frac{\lambda}{4\mu_f}$
- Refractive index condition: $\mu_a < \mu_f < \mu_g$

## Polarization

- Malus's law: $I = I_0\cos^2\theta$
- Amplitude: $E = E_0\cos\theta$
- Circular polarization: equal amplitudes, phase difference $\pi/2$
- Elliptical polarization: unequal amplitudes, phase difference $\pi/2$ or other nonzero phase shift

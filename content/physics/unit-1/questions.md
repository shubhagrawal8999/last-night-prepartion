# Likely Questions

1. Define interference and distinguish between constructive and destructive interference.
2. Derive the condition for bright and dark fringes in a thin film in the reflected system.
3. Explain the origin of the additional $\lambda/2$ phase change in thin-film reflection.
4. Derive the fringe width formula for a wedge-shaped film.
5. Explain the working of an anti-reflection coating and derive its minimum thickness.
6. Differentiate between unpolarized, plane polarized, circularly polarized, and elliptically polarized light.
7. State and prove Malus's law.
8. Describe how an air wedge is used to test optical flatness.
9. Explain the phenomenon of double refraction in anisotropic crystals.
10. A thin film is illuminated by monochromatic light. Derive the condition for minima and maxima in reflected light.

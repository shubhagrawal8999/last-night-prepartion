# Likely Exam Questions

> These are **practice prompts for the demo topic**, not predictions about your exam.

1. Differentiate a polynomial such as $y=3x^4-5x^2+2x-8$.
2. Find the gradient of $y=x^2+2x$ at $x=2$.
3. Differentiate a product such as $y=x^2\sin x$.
4. State what the derivative represents geometrically at a point on a curve.
5. Find and classify the stationary points of a simple polynomial (if this is in your course).
